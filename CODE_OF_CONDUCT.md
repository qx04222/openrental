# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other end is trying.

## What that means here

**Expected:** giving feedback on the code rather than the person; saying "this
breaks X because Y" instead of "this is wrong"; accepting that a maintainer may
say no; accepting that a contributor may not have time to finish.

**Not accepted:** personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language,
sustained disruption, publishing someone's private information, or persistently
re-litigating a decision that has been made and explained.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on
this repository, or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts. For
serious or repeated behaviour, a block is permanent and does not require prior
warning.

Maintainers who do not hold themselves to this are subject to it too.
